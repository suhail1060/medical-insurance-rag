Coordination of Benefits (COB) prevents duplicate payments and establishes the payment hierarchy when an insured individual is covered simultaneously by more than one group health benefit plan. Apex Health Plans applies standard National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) COB guidelines.

Hierarchy for Primary Versus Secondary Payer Determination:
1. Non-Dependent Versus Dependent Rule:
A plan that covers an individual as an active employee, member, or subscriber is Primary over a plan that covers that same individual as a dependent (such as a covered spouse).

2. Active Versus Inactive / Retired Employee Rule:
A plan covering an individual as an active, full-time employee pays primary before a plan covering that same individual as a laid-off, retired, or COBRA-enrolled beneficiary.

3. Dependent Children and the 'Birthday Rule':
For dependent children covered under both parents' health insurance policies:
- Primary Payer: The plan of the parent whose calendar birthday (month and day, excluding year) falls earlier in the calendar year is the primary payer.
- Secondary Payer: The plan of the parent whose birthday falls later in the year is secondary.
- Same Birthday: If both parents share the same birth month and day, the plan that has covered a parent for the longer continuous period is primary.
- Court Decrees: In cases of divorce or legal separation, a court decree assigning healthcare financial responsibility supersedes the Birthday Rule.

Secondary Payer Calculation Methodology:
When Apex Health Plans functions as the secondary payer:
- The primary insurer processes the claim first, paying according to its benefit terms and generating an Explanation of Benefits (EOB).
- The claim and primary EOB are submitted to Apex.
- Apex calculates what it would have paid if it were primary.
- Apex reimburses the remaining unpaid member cost-sharing balance (deductibles, coinsurance, copays), up to the contractual Allowed Amount. The combined total payment will not exceed 100% of the allowable healthcare expenses.
