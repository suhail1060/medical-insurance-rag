Apex Health Plans processes and reimburses healthcare claims in strict compliance with state and federal Prompt Pay statutes. This policy specifies processing turnaround expectations and member reimbursement schedules.

Definition of a Clean Claim:
A 'Clean Claim' is defined as a claim submitted via standard electronic (EDI 837) or paper (CMS-1500/UB-04) format that contains all required patient identification, valid diagnosis and procedure codes, provider tax identification, and complete supporting documentation without requiring additional external clinical records or manual investigative intervention.

Statutory Adjudication Timelines:
1. Electronic Clean Claims:
- Adjudication occurs within fourteen (14) calendar days of electronic receipt.
- Reimbursement via Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) is deposited into the healthcare provider's or member's designated banking account within three (3) business days following adjudication.

2. Paper Clean Claims:
- Paper claims submitted by postal mail are scanned and adjudicated within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt.
- Reimbursement checks and physical Explanations of Benefits (EOBs) are mailed via first-class USPS within five (5) business days following adjudication.

3. Deficient or Pended Claims:
If a claim lacks required information, a formal Notice of Claim Deficiency (EDI 277 or written request) is issued within twenty-one (21) days of submission. The submitter has forty-five (45) days to provide the requested information. Once received, the claim is adjudicated within fifteen (15) days.

Late Payment Interest Penalties:
In accordance with statutory prompt pay rules, any clean claim remaining unpaid past the 30-day (paper) or 14-day (electronic) deadline accrues mandatory statutory interest payable to the provider or member. Interest is calculated at a rate of 1.0% per month (12% per annum) or the maximum state statutory rate, compounded daily from the date payment became overdue.
