A copayment is a fixed, mandatory dollar fee payable by the insured member directly to a participating medical provider at the exact time covered healthcare services are delivered. Copayments do not require prior satisfaction of the annual calendar deductible and are applied on a per-encounter or per-date-of-service basis across designated healthcare delivery tiers.

Under the Apex PPO Gold Plan, in-network copayments are structured as follows:
- Primary Care Physician (PCP) routine office visits, preventive consultations, and general medical examinations: $25 per visit.
- Specialist physician consultations (including cardiology, dermatology, orthopedic evaluation, and endocrinology): $50 per visit.
- Retail walk-in convenient care clinics: $15 per visit.
- Urgent care center visits for acute, non-life-threatening injuries or illnesses: $75 per visit.
- Emergency room facility evaluation: $250 per visit. If the insured member is formally admitted to an acute inpatient hospital bed within twenty-four (24) hours of the initial emergency room triage, the $250 emergency copayment is waived entirely and replaced by applicable inpatient hospital deductible and coinsurance provisions.

For members enrolled in the Apex HMO Silver Plan, fixed copayments are set at $35 for primary care consultations and $70 for designated specialist appointments. Specialist visits under HMO contracts strictly require an active, validated electronic referral submitted by the assigned primary care physician prior to the appointment date.

Fixed copayments contribute directly toward the member's Annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum. However, copayments do not reduce or credit against the annual deductible. If a member receives both an office evaluation and separate diagnostic surgical procedures (e.g., in-office biopsy or specialized joint injection) during a single visit, distinct cost-sharing obligations may apply for each discrete service code.
