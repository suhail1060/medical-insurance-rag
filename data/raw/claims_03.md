When a healthcare claim is adjudicated by Apex Health Plans, decisions resulting in non-payment or cost-sharing reallocations are communicated via standardized Claim Adjustment Reason Codes (CARC) and Remittance Advice Remark Codes (RARC). Understanding these codes facilitates prompt claim resolution.

Common CARC Denial Codes and Corrective Actions:

1. Code CO-16 (Claim/service lacks information or has submission errors):
- Meaning: The claim is missing vital clinical or administrative data, such as an invalid NPI, missing diagnosis pointer, or absent date of service.
- Resolution: Resubmit a corrected claim with the missing procedural details within 60 days.

2. Code CO-50 (Non-covered services because not deemed medically necessary):
- Meaning: The medical management division determined that the procedure does not meet clinical criteria under evidence-based guidelines.
- Resolution: File a Level 1 Appeal within 180 days with supporting clinical documentation, operative notes, and a physician's Letter of Medical Necessity.

3. Code CO-29 (Timely filing limit expired):
- Meaning: The claim was submitted after the plan's filing deadline (90 days for in-network providers; 180 days for member claims).
- Resolution: Provide electronic clearinghouse audit logs or postal certified mail receipts proving timely original transmission.

4. Code CO-97 (The benefit for this service is included in the payment for another service):
- Meaning: Unbundling denial. The submitted service code is considered integral to the primary comprehensive surgical or clinical code.
- Resolution: Provider must append an appropriate clinical modifier (e.g., -59 or -X{EPSU}) if the service represents an independent, distinct procedure.

5. Code PR-1, PR-2, PR-3 (Patient Responsibility: Deductible, Coinsurance, Copay):
- Meaning: Normal cost-sharing reallocation assigned to the member.
- Resolution: Billed to the insured member in accordance with plan benefit schedules.

6. Code CO-197 (Precertification/prior authorization was not obtained):
- Meaning: The service required prior approval that was not secured before treatment.
- Resolution: Submit a retrospective authorization request along with evidence of urgent circumstances.
