The Annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum (OOPM) represents the upper financial limit of cost-sharing a member is required to pay for covered Essential Health Benefits during a single 12-month calendar benefit period. Once a member's eligible payments equal the specified out-of-pocket threshold, Apex Health Plans reimburses 100% of the Allowed Amount for all covered in-network medical and prescription services for the remainder of that calendar year.

For the active policy year, out-of-pocket maximum caps comply with Affordable Care Act statutory limits:
- Apex PPO Gold Plan: $6,500 for individual coverage; $13,000 for family coverage.
- Apex HMO Silver Plan: $8,500 for individual coverage; $17,000 for family coverage.
- Apex Bronze HDHP Plan: $7,200 for individual coverage; $14,400 for family coverage.

The following member payments accumulate toward the Annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum:
1. Satisfied annual calendar deductibles.
2. In-network primary care, specialist, urgent care, and emergency room copayments.
3. In-network coinsurance percentages paid for inpatient hospital admissions, surgical operations, diagnostic imaging, and therapeutic infusions.
4. Prescription drug copayments and pharmacy coinsurance under Tiers 1 through 4.

The following expenses do not count toward the Out-of-Pocket Maximum under any circumstances:
- Monthly plan premium installments.
- Penalties assessed for failure to obtain mandatory prior authorization.
- Balance-billed charges submitted by out-of-network medical facilities exceeding allowable limits.
- Elective, non-covered, or contractually excluded healthcare procedures (e.g., adult cosmetic surgery or non-formulary pharmaceuticals).

For family plans with embedded provisions, an individual family member who reaches the individual maximum ($6,500 on PPO Gold) qualifies for 100% plan reimbursement immediately, even if the total family maximum ($13,000) has not yet been satisfied across other enrolled dependents.
