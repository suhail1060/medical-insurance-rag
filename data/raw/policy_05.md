Apex Health Plans establishes contractual provider networks to control healthcare costs and ensure credentialed quality of care. Medical practitioners and healthcare facilities are grouped into distinct network classifications with varying cost-sharing implications.

1. Tier 1 / In-Network Participating Providers: These physicians, healthcare systems, surgical centers, and diagnostic laboratories have entered into binding contractual agreements with Apex Health Plans. Participating providers agree to accept negotiated discounted payment rates—termed the 'Allowed Amount'—as full reimbursement for covered clinical services. Participating providers are legally prohibited from balance billing members for any difference between their standard retail charge and the contracted rate. Members pay only contractual copayments, deductibles, and coinsurance.

2. Out-of-Network / Non-Participating Providers: These practitioners and medical facilities maintain no contractual relationship with Apex Health Plans. Under the Apex HMO Silver Plan, healthcare received from non-participating providers is strictly excluded from coverage (0% plan reimbursement), rendering the member responsible for 100% of all billed costs, unless the care involves emergency stabilization.

Under the Apex PPO Gold Plan, out-of-network care is covered at reduced benefit levels. Non-emergency out-of-network services are subject to a separate $3,000 individual out-of-network deductible, followed by a 50% coinsurance requirement based on the Usual, Customary, and Reasonable (UCR) fee benchmark. Non-participating providers reserve the legal right to bill the insured member for any dollar amounts remaining above the UCR threshold.

Emergency medical conditions, as defined under federal law, are covered at the in-network cost-sharing level regardless of the participating status of the hospital facility or treating emergency physician.
