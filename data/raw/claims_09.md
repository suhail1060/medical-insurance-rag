This policy establishes mandatory timely filing standards for initial and retroactive claim submissions, defining required filing windows, acceptable proof of timely transmission, and criteria for Good Cause exceptions.

Standard Timely Filing Limits:
- Participating In-Network Providers: Claims must be submitted within ninety (90) calendar days from the date of service.
- Out-of-Network Providers and Member Reimbursement Claims: Claims must be submitted within one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from the date of service.
- Coordination of Benefits Secondary Claims: Claims submitted to Apex Health Plans as secondary payer must be filed within ninety (90) calendar days from the date printed on the primary insurer's Explanation of Benefits (EOB).

Claims submitted after these deadlines will be rejected with CARC Code CO-29 (Timely filing limit expired). Participating providers cannot bill the member for claims denied due to provider untimely filing.

Proof of Timely Submission:
To overturn an untimely filing denial, submitters must provide verifiable documentation showing that the claim was initially filed within the applicable deadline:
1. Electronic Clearinghouse 999 or 277CA acceptance reports showing successful payer receipt.
2. Certified mail return receipt signed by an Apex mailroom representative.
3. System-generated Member Portal upload confirmation receipt containing a valid tracking number.

Good Cause Exceptions:
Timely filing limits may be extended up to a maximum of three hundred sixty-five (365) calendar days from the date of service under documented 'Good Cause' circumstances:
- Documented physical or mental incapacitation of the insured member during the filing window.
- Retroactive enrollment determinations resulting from state Medicaid eligibility decisions or employer administrative delays.
- Subrogation settlements involving third-party motor vehicle liability litigation.
