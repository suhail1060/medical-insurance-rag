This contract explicitly excludes coverage for medical procedures, treatments, pharmaceuticals, devices, and hospital services that do not meet defined standards of medical necessity or fall outside covered health categories.

Key policy exclusions include:
1. Cosmetic Surgery and Aesthetic Alterations: Any surgical intervention or dermatological treatment performed primarily to improve, alter, or enhance physical appearance without restoring a bodily function impaired by trauma, congenital defect, or disease is excluded. Excluded procedures include elective rhinoplasty, rhytidectomy (face lift), liposuction, breast augmentation, chemical skin peeling, and hair restoration. Exception: In accordance with the Women's Health and Cancer Rights Act (WHCRA), breast reconstruction and symmetry surgery following a medically necessary mastectomy are fully covered.

2. Experimental, Investigational, and Unproven Treatments: Services, drugs, medical devices, or clinical protocols that are not approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the specific therapeutic indication, or that fail to demonstrate proven efficacy across well-designed, randomized, peer-reviewed clinical trials indexed in MEDLINE, are non-covered. Participation in Phase I and Phase II clinical trials is excluded, whereas qualifying routine patient care costs incurred during approved Phase III and IV oncology trials are covered in accordance with federal mandates.

3. General Non-Covered Items: Routine adult dental examinations, cleanings, and restorative prosthetics (unless covered under a separate dental rider); adult vision refraction exams and corrective refractive laser surgeries (LASIK/PRK); private duty nursing; over-the-counter vitamins and nutritional supplements; and non-medical custodial care in skilled nursing homes.

Expenses arising from contractually excluded services do not qualify for plan benefits and do not accrue toward deductible or out-of-pocket maximum calculations.
