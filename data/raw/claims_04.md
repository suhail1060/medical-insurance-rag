This protocol defines the administrative dispute framework for challenging adverse benefit determinations, partial claim reductions, or service denials issued by Apex Health Plans.

Level 1: Internal Administrative Appeal:
1. Submission Window: The member, an authorized representative, or the treating physician acting on the member's behalf must submit a written appeal within one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from the date of the Explanation of Benefits (EOB).
2. Required Documentation: The appeal packet must include:
   - Apex Formal Member Appeal Form (Form AP-101).
   - Copy of the disputed EOB and original claim.
   - Comprehensive physician letter detailing clinical medical necessity.
   - Relevant diagnostic lab reports, imaging scans, and medical history.
3. Adjudication Turnaround Timelines:
   - Expedited / Urgent Appeals: Decided within seventy-two (72) hours if the treating clinician certifies that delaying treatment would cause severe pain or endanger health.
   - Standard Pre-Service Appeals: Decided within thirty (30) calendar days.
   - Standard Post-Service Claims: Decided within sixty (60) calendar days.
   All Level 1 appeals are reviewed by medical professionals who were not involved in the initial denial determination and who practice in the same or similar clinical specialty.

Level 2: Independent External Review:
If the internal appeal upholds the denial on grounds involving medical necessity, clinical appropriateness, experimental treatment exclusions, or emergency coverage, the member has four (4) months from the final internal determination to request an Independent External Review.
- Assignment: The case is assigned to an accredited Independent Review Organization (IRO) through state insurance department portals.
- Binding Decision: Standard external reviews are completed within forty-five (45) calendar days; expedited external reviews are completed within seventy-two (72) hours. The IRO's decision is legally binding on Apex Health Plans. If overturned, the plan must process and pay the claim within forty-eight (48) hours.
