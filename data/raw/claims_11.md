Apex Health Plans operates an expedited claims adjudication protocol for healthcare services arising from acute medical emergencies, trauma center hospitalizations, and urgent medical interventions.

Criteria for Expedited Adjudication:
An expedited claim review is triggered when:
- The claim involves emergency room care and trauma stabilization governed by the Prudent Layperson Standard.
- The claim involves an urgent inpatient admission directly from the emergency department.
- A treating physician certifies in writing that standard 30-day adjudication timelines would compromise patient clinical stability or interrupt life-sustaining therapies.

Operational Processing Workflow:
1. Automated Prioritization: Claims submitted with Place of Service Code 23 (Emergency Room) or revenue codes 0450-0459 (Emergency Room Hospital) bypass standard routine batches and route to the Specialized Emergency Adjudication Team.
2. 72-Hour Adjudication Standard: Complete emergency clean claims are adjudicated within seventy-two (72) hours of electronic receipt.
3. Waiver of Prior Authorization: All medical necessity and prior authorization rules are waived for acute emergency stabilization services rendered prior to patient stabilization.
4. Automatic Inpatient Copay Adjustment: If the emergency encounter leads to an acute inpatient admission within twenty-four (24) hours, automated logic waives the $250 emergency facility copayment and routes the claim to the inpatient hospital benefit tier.
5. Discharge Coordination Support: The expedited team works alongside hospital trauma case managers to ensure post-stabilization transfers to in-network rehabilitation facilities are approved within twenty-four (24) hours of the initial clinical referral.

Members and providers can request expedited review by contacting the 24/7 Clinical Emergency Review Desk at 1-800-555-EMER.
