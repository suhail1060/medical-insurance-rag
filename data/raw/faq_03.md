Question: How can I verify that a physician, specialist, hospital, or diagnostic center is participating in my plan's in-network directory?

Answer: Choosing in-network medical providers is essential to avoid unexpected medical expenses and balance billing. You can verify network participation using the following three official methods:

1. Online Provider Finder Portal:
Log into your member account at www.apexhealthplans.com/find-care. The online directory is updated weekly and filters providers by your specific plan network (e.g., Apex Select HMO vs Apex Choice PPO). Search by doctor name, medical specialty, geographic radius, or hospital affiliation. Always confirm that the specific facility location where you will receive care is listed as active.

2. Direct Provider Confirmation:
When contacting a doctor's office, do not simply ask: 'Do you take Apex insurance?' Most offices accept payments from major insurers. Instead, ask specifically: 'Are you a participating in-network provider for the Apex Choice PPO Network for this tax identification number and facility address?' Request the physician's NPI (National Provider Identifier) to cross-reference with Member Services.

3. Telephone Verification via Member Services:
Call the customer service phone number located on the back of your Member ID card (1-800-555-APEX). A customer service representative will confirm the network status of the practitioner, check whether secondary facility providers (such as anesthesiologists or pathologists) at that hospital are in-network, and provide you with a unique Directory Inquiry Reference Number. 

Important Hospital Caution: Even if a hospital facility is fully in-network, certain contracted specialists working inside the hospital—such as emergency physicians, radiologists, and anesthesiologists—may be independent contractors. Federal No Surprises Act rules protect you against surprise billing for non-consensual out-of-network emergency and ancillary services at in-network facilities.
