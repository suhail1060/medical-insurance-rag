Question: What is prior authorization, why is it required, and which medical services require it?

Answer: Prior authorization (also known as precertification or pre-approval) is a formal clinical evaluation conducted by Apex Health Plans before you receive specific non-emergency medical services. Its purpose is to verify that the proposed treatment, diagnostic test, or medication is medically necessary, clinically appropriate, and cost-effective under evidence-based guidelines.

When is Prior Authorization Required?
Prior authorization is mandatory for high-cost, specialized, or elective healthcare services, including:
- Inpatient hospital admissions and surgical hospitalizations.
- Advanced outpatient diagnostic imaging: MRI, CT, and PET scans.
- Outpatient surgical procedures performed at ambulatory surgery centers.
- Specialty biotechnology drugs, biologic therapies, and chemotherapy infusions.
- Durable medical equipment (DME) items with a purchase cost exceeding $750 (such as motorized wheelchairs or specialized hospital beds).
- Outpatient physical, occupational, and speech therapy after reaching twenty (20) visits in a calendar year.

How Does the Process Work?
Your treating physician's office is responsible for initiating the prior authorization request by submitting clinical records, diagnosis codes (ICD-10), and procedure codes (CPT) via the secure Apex Provider Portal.
- Standard Requests: Reviewed and decided within fourteen (14) calendar days.
- Urgent Requests: If waiting fourteen days would seriously jeopardize your life or health, your physician can request an expedited review, which is decided within seventy-two (72) hours.

What Happens If Prior Authorization Is Not Obtained?
If a service requiring prior authorization is performed without approval, the claim will be denied. In-network providers cannot bill you for services denied due to their failure to obtain required prior authorization. However, if you see an out-of-network provider, you may be held financially responsible for the entire bill.
