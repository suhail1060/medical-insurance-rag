This section defines the annual deductible obligations applicable to covered members under Apex Health Plans for the standard calendar benefit year (January 1 through December 31). The deductible represents the predetermined cumulative dollar amount a member must pay out-of-pocket for covered medical services before the plan commences cost-sharing or coinsurance reimbursements.

Under the Apex PPO Gold Plan, the individual in-network deductible is established at $1,500 per calendar year, while the family coverage deductible is established at $3,000. Under the Apex HMO Silver Plan, the individual deductible is $3,000 and the family deductible is $6,000. For high-deductible health plans such as the Apex Bronze HDHP, the individual deductible is $5,500 and the family deductible is $11,000. 

Family policies utilize an embedded deductible mechanism. Under this structure, once any individual enrolled under a family contract satisfies the individual deductible threshold ($1,500 on PPO Gold), the plan will initiate coinsurance benefits for that specific individual, regardless of whether the collective $3,000 family threshold has been satisfied. Once aggregate qualified medical expenses paid across all family members equal the total family deductible, all enrolled family members receive full plan benefits.

Expenses that credit toward the annual deductible include eligible inpatient room and board, outpatient surgical fees, diagnostic radiology (MRI, CT scans), and emergency ambulance transport. Routine preventive services mandated under Section 2713 of the Public Health Service Act—such as annual wellness physicals, well-child visits, and standard immunizations—are fully exempt from the deductible and covered at 100% in-network. Monthly premiums, copayments for routine office visits, non-covered elective procedures, and balance-billed charges from non-participating healthcare providers do not credit toward the deductible.
