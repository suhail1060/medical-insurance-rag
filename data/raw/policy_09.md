Apex Health Plans provides comprehensive coverage for behavioral healthcare, psychiatric evaluation, psychological counseling, and substance use disorder treatment in full compliance with the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA) and applicable state parity statutes.

Financial and Quantitative Parity Standards:
The financial requirements (copayments, coinsurance, and deductibles) and quantitative treatment limitations (such as annual visit caps or day limits) applied to mental health and substance abuse disorders are no more restrictive than those applied to general medical and surgical benefits across corresponding classifications.
- Outpatient Psychotherapy and Counseling: Individual, family, and group psychotherapy sessions conducted by licensed clinical psychologists, psychiatrists, or licensed clinical social workers (LCSWs) are subject to a flat $25 copayment per session on the Apex PPO Gold Plan, identical to a primary care office visit. No arbitrary annual maximum session limit is imposed.
- Inpatient Psychiatric and Residential Addiction Treatment: Inpatient behavioral admissions are covered under the standard acute hospital cost-sharing tier (deductible plus 20% coinsurance on PPO Gold; 30% coinsurance on HMO Silver).

Non-Quantitative Treatment Limitations (NQTL):
Apex Health Plans ensures that clinical utilization review protocols, medical necessity criteria, step-therapy algorithms, and credentialing standards for mental health services are developed and administered equivalently to medical/surgical care. Medically necessary detoxification, residential stabilization, partial hospitalization programs (PHP), and intensive outpatient programs (IOP) are evaluated using objective, peer-reviewed clinical guidelines (such as ASAM criteria for substance abuse and APA guidelines for psychiatric disorders). Prior authorization may be required for non-emergency inpatient psychiatric admissions, but cannot be required for emergency acute stabilization.
