Prior authorization (also referred to as precertification or pre-service clinical review) is a mandatory utilization management process used by Apex Health Plans to verify the medical necessity, clinical appropriateness, and safety of designated high-cost medical treatments prior to their delivery.

Prior authorization is mandatory for the following categories of services:
- Non-emergency inpatient hospital admissions and residential rehabilitation stays.
- Advanced outpatient diagnostic imaging, including Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), Positron Emission Tomography (PET scans), and Computed Tomography (CT scans).
- Elective orthopedic, spinal, and cardiovascular surgical procedures.
- Specialty biotechnology pharmaceuticals, chemotherapy infusions, and gene therapy regimens.
- Durable medical equipment purchases exceeding an individual purchase cost of $750 or continuous rental exceeding $150 per month.

The ordering physician must submit clinical documentation—including pertinent history, physical notes, laboratory findings, conservative therapy records, and specific ICD-10 diagnostic codes—via the Apex Provider Portal or via secure EDI 278 transactions. 

Review Timelines:
- Standard Pre-Service Requests: A formal written determination is issued within fourteen (14) calendar days of receiving a complete clinical packet.
- Urgent / Expedited Requests: When a standard review timeline could seriously jeopardize the member's life, physical health, or ability to regain maximum function, a determination is rendered within seventy-two (72) hours.

Failure to secure prior authorization before elective services are performed will result in a total denial of insurance coverage or a mandatory 50% administrative benefit reduction, with financial liability reassigned to the provider (for in-network care) or the member (for out-of-network care).
