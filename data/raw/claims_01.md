This standard operating procedure outlines the formal protocol for filing manual medical claims under Apex Health Plans when services are rendered by non-participating healthcare providers or when upfront payment is required at the time of care.

1. Timely Filing Threshold:
Manual claims submitted by policyholders must be postmarked or electronically uploaded within one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from the date services were delivered. Claims received after the 180-day deadline will be rejected for untimely filing without eligibility for standard administrative reconsideration.

2. Submission Phases:
- Phase 1: Data Assembly: The member must collect a completed CMS-1500 (or UB-04 for facility services) itemized billing ledger from the servicing medical provider. The document must display the provider's 10-digit National Provider Identifier (NPI), Tax Identification Number (TIN), five-digit CPT/HCPCS procedure codes, valid ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes, date of service, and itemized unit charges. Cash register receipts and credit card charge slips are not sufficient.
- Phase 2: Member Claim Form Completion: The member must complete the Apex Health Plans Member Claim Reimbursement Form (Form MP-202). All fields—including Member Policy ID, Patient Full Legal Name, Group Number, and Other Insurance Coverage Declarations—must be filled out accurately.
- Phase 3: Transmission: 
  * Electronic Submission (Recommended): Upload high-resolution PDF scans through the Member Portal (www.apexhealthplans.com/claims/upload). A confirmation tracking number is issued within 60 seconds.
  * Postal Mail Submission: Mail original claim documentation to: Apex Health Plans, Member Claims Operations, P.O. Box 41200, Omaha, NE 68145.

3. Processing and Notification:
Claims entering the adjudication pipeline undergo automated eligibility verification and medical coding checks. Members receive an electronic or paper Explanation of Benefits (EOB) within thirty (30) business days detailing the claim determination, contractual adjustments, plan payment, and member cost-sharing obligations.
