Question: What is the process and timeframe for adding a newborn baby, newly adopted child, or new spouse to my existing health insurance policy?

Answer: Adding a dependent to your health insurance policy outside the standard Annual Open Enrollment window requires a Qualifying Life Event (QLE), which triggers a thirty (30) day Special Enrollment Period (SEP).

1. Adding a Newborn or Adopted Child:
- Automatic 31-Day Coverage Window: Under Apex Health Plans, a newborn child is automatically covered for the first thirty-one (31) days of life under the mother's or policyholder's contract for hospital nursery care, routine newborn exams, and emergency care.
- Mandatory Enrollment Deadline: To ensure uninterrupted coverage beyond the 31st day, you must submit a formal Dependent Enrollment Request within thirty (30) calendar days of the birth or formal adoption placement. If you miss this 30-day window, coverage terminates on day 32, and you cannot add the child until the next Open Enrollment period.
- Required Documentation: You must provide a certified state birth certificate, certificate of live birth signed by the delivering hospital, or official legal adoption documentation. Coverage is applied retroactively to the exact date of birth.

2. Adding a New Spouse:
- Marriage Event Deadline: You must notify Member Services or your employer's human resources department within thirty (30) calendar days of your legal marriage date.
- Required Documentation: You must submit a valid marriage certificate issued by a government authority along with the signed enrollment form.
- Effective Date: Coverage for a new spouse typically becomes effective on the first day of the month following receipt of your complete enrollment application.

Adjustments to monthly premiums reflecting the transition from individual to employee-plus-spouse, employee-plus-child, or family coverage will be applied retroactively to the qualifying event date.
