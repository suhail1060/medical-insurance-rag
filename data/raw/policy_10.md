Apex Health Plans covers routine and high-risk maternity care, labor and delivery hospitalization, and immediate postpartum healthcare for eligible members and covered spouses in accordance with state and federal healthcare standards.

Prenatal and Postnatal Care:
Routine outpatient prenatal checkups, maternal blood panels, gestational diabetes screenings, routine fetal ultrasounds, and postpartum examinations rendered by in-network obstetricians, gynecologists, or certified nurse-midwives are classified as preventive services. These routine services are reimbursed at 100% with no deductible, copayment, or coinsurance obligations. Specialized diagnostic evaluations, such as non-routine amniocentesis, chorionic villus sampling (CVS), or non-routine high-risk fetal monitoring, are subject to standard outpatient diagnostic cost-sharing.

Inpatient Labor and Delivery:
Hospital confinement for childbirth is covered subject to standard inpatient facility cost-sharing (annual deductible followed by 20% coinsurance under the Apex PPO Gold Plan). In compliance with the federal Newborns' and Mothers' Health Protection Act (NMHPA), the plan guarantees minimum inpatient coverage of:
- Forty-eight (48) hours following an uncomplicated vaginal delivery.
- Ninety-six (96) hours following a cesarean section delivery.
An attending obstetrician cannot be required to obtain prior authorization for prescribing hospitalization up to these minimum timeframes.

Newborn Enrollment Provisions:
A newborn infant born to a covered policyholder is automatically covered under the policy for the first thirty-one (31) calendar days following birth for routine nursery care, neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) stays, and mandatory newborn metabolic screenings. To continue health coverage beyond the initial 31-day window, the policyholder must formally notify Apex Member Services and submit an enrollment change request along with an official birth certificate or hospital statement of live birth within 30 days of delivery.
