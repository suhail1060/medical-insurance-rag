Question: How does my health insurance plan cover medical care when I travel within the United States or abroad in a foreign country?

Answer: Health insurance coverage while traveling depends heavily on whether your travel is domestic or international, and whether the medical need is routine or an emergency:

1. Domestic Travel Within the United States:
- Emergency Care: Emergency room treatment for sudden, life-threatening conditions is covered at in-network benefit levels at any hospital emergency facility in all 50 states, pursuant to the federal No Surprises Act. Your cost-sharing is limited to your standard in-network copay ($250 on Apex PPO Gold).
- Urgent Care: If you are enrolled in an Apex PPO Plan, urgent care visits are covered nationwide through our reciprocal national network alliance. If you are enrolled in an HMO plan, out-of-area urgent care is covered only for acute conditions requiring immediate attention when you cannot safely travel back to your local network.
- Routine Care: Elective physical exams, ongoing prescription maintenance, and routine specialist visits outside your plan's home network area are not covered on HMO plans and are subject to high out-of-network cost-sharing on PPO plans.

2. International Travel Abroad:
- Routine Medical Services: Standard commercial policies provide 0% coverage for routine, scheduled, or elective medical care outside the United States.
- Emergency Medical Treatment: Apex Health Plans provides coverage for acute emergency hospital treatment while traveling abroad, reimbursed on an out-of-network basis. Foreign medical facilities will not bill Apex directly; you must pay the foreign provider out-of-pocket and submit a foreign claim reimbursement packet upon your return. The packet must include translated medical records, itemized currency breakdowns, and proof of payment.
- Exclusions: International medical evacuation, air ambulance repatriation to the United States, and travel cancellation are strictly excluded. We strongly recommend purchasing standalone international travel medical insurance for overseas trips.
