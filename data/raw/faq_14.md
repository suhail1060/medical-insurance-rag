Question: What clinical benefits, medication cost reductions, and care coordination services are available for managing chronic health conditions?

Answer: Apex Health Plans provides comprehensive Chronic Disease Management Programs designed to help members effectively manage long-term medical conditions, prevent acute complications, and reduce overall healthcare costs. These programs cover conditions including Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), asthma, congestive heart failure (CHF), and hypertension.

Key Chronic Coverage Features:
1. Low-Cost and Zero-Cost Preventive Maintenance Medications:
Under the Apex PPO Gold and Silver HMO plans, essential generic maintenance medications for blood pressure regulation (such as Lisinopril and Amlodipine), cholesterol management (such as Atorvastatin), and standard asthma inhalers are covered with a $0 copayment and are exempt from the annual deductible.
2. Specialized Diabetes Care Benefits:
- Formulary preferred insulin products are capped at a maximum member copayment of $35 per 30-day supply.
- Blood glucose meters, diabetic test strips, and lancets are covered under the pharmacy benefit with a $10 copayment.
- Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs, such as Dexcom or FreeStyle Libre) and automated insulin pumps are covered under Durable Medical Equipment (DME) with prior clinical authorization, subject to a 20% coinsurance after deductible.
3. Dedicated Nurse Care Coordination:
Members diagnosed with qualifying chronic conditions are paired with a licensed Registered Nurse Care Manager at no additional charge. Care managers assist with medication adherence, post-hospital discharge transitions, appointment scheduling, and dietary coordination.
4. Outpatient Education and Nutritional Counseling:
Members receive up to six (6) covered outpatient nutritional counseling and diabetes self-management education visits per calendar year with a $0 copayment when referred by a network primary care provider.
