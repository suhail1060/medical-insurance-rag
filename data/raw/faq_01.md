Question: How do I submit a medical insurance claim for reimbursement when my doctor does not bill the insurance company directly?

Answer: When you receive care from an in-network healthcare provider, the doctor or hospital will submit the claim directly to Apex Health Plans on your behalf. However, if you visit an out-of-network provider or pay for covered medical services out-of-pocket, you must submit a manual member claim within one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from the date of service.

To submit a claim, follow these steps:
1. Obtain an Itemized Statement: Request a formal itemized medical bill from your provider. A standard credit card receipt or balance-due invoice is not acceptable. The itemized bill must contain:
   - Provider's full legal name, facility address, and National Provider Identifier (NPI).
   - Diagnosis codes (ICD-10-CM).
   - Procedure billing codes (CPT or HCPCS codes) for each discrete service.
   - Itemized charge for each specific procedure.
   - Date and place of service.
2. Complete the Member Claim Form: Download the Apex CMS-1500 Member Reimbursement Form from the online portal (www.apexhealthplans.com/forms). Fill out the patient name, policy ID number, date of birth, and group number.
3. Attach Proof of Payment: Include a copy of your cancelled check, credit card receipt, or paid invoice showing payment in full.
4. Submit the Claim: Upload digital scans of your completed claim packet via the secure Member Portal under 'Submit a Claim', or mail the physical documents to: Apex Health Plans Claims Adjudication, P.O. Box 41200, Omaha, NE 68145.

Standard claim processing requires approximately thirty (30) business days from receipt of a complete filing. Once processed, an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) and reimbursement check or direct deposit will be issued.
