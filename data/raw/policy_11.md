Emergency medical services are covered twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week, anywhere in the world, without prior authorization. Coverage determinations for emergency care are strictly governed by the 'Prudent Layperson Standard' under federal law.

An Emergency Medical Condition is defined as an acute medical or behavioral condition exhibiting symptoms of sufficient severity (including severe pain) such that an average person with an average knowledge of health and medicine could reasonably expect the absence of immediate medical attention to result in:
1. Placing the health of the individual (or an unborn child) in serious jeopardy;
2. Serious impairment to bodily functions; or
3. Serious dysfunction of any bodily organ or part.

Emergency Cost-Sharing and No Surprises Act Protections:
- Emergency Room Copayment: Enrolled members are subject to a flat $250 copayment per emergency room encounter under the Apex PPO Gold Plan, plus applicable coinsurance after deductible for advanced imaging and surgical procedures performed during the emergency stay. The $250 facility copayment is waived if the member is admitted directly into an acute hospital bed within 24 hours.
- Out-of-Network Emergency Protections: Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency services provided by out-of-network emergency facilities or out-of-network emergency physicians are reimbursed without requiring prior authorization. Member cost-sharing cannot exceed the in-network copayment and coinsurance rates, and payments count directly toward in-network deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums. Balance billing by non-participating emergency providers is prohibited by law.

Ambulance Transport: Medically necessary ground ambulance transport to the nearest qualified acute hospital is covered at 20% coinsurance after deductible on PPO Gold. Emergency air ambulance is subject to statutory dispute arbitration provisions under the No Surprises Act.
