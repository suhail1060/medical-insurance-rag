The Apex Health Plans Special Investigation Unit (SIU) is responsible for detecting, preventing, and investigating healthcare fraud, waste, and abuse (FWA). The SIU utilizes automated machine learning algorithms, statistical claim modeling, and clinical audits to identify irregular billing patterns.

Key Fraud and Abuse Audit Triggers:
1. Unbundling of Healthcare Services:
Submitting fragmented, separate CPT procedure codes for clinical components that are properly billed under a single, comprehensive procedure code (e.g., billing individual incision, surgical excision, and closure codes rather than a single comprehensive surgical code) to generate inflated reimbursements.

2. Upcoding:
Billing for a higher-level, more complex evaluation and management (E/M) service or surgical procedure code than is supported by actual medical record documentation. Examples include routinely billing Level 5 comprehensive office visits (CPT 99215) for brief routine follow-ups.

3. Phantom Billing and Services Not Rendered:
Billing for diagnostic tests, medical office appointments, physical therapy sessions, or durable medical equipment that were never provided to the member.

4. Duplicate Billing:
Submitting duplicate claims for the exact same service, patient, and date of service under altered procedural modifiers, separate tax identifiers, or consecutive dates.

5. Outlier Prescription Dispensing:
Pharmacy claims flagged for excessive quantities of controlled substances, compounding kits, or high-cost specialty drugs that exceed clinical dosing norms.

Investigation and Recovery Protocol:
When a claim or provider profile is flagged:
- The SIU issues a formal Records Request requiring full medical charts within thirty (30) calendar days.
- Claims associated with the investigated provider may be placed on prepayment clinical review.
- Confirmed fraud cases result in immediate claim denials, retroactive recoupment demands, provider contract termination, and referral to state insurance fraud bureaus and the federal Office of Inspector General (OIG).
