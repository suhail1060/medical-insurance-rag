Question: What is the grace period if I miss my monthly insurance premium payment, and when will my policy be cancelled?

Answer: Missing a monthly health insurance premium payment triggers a formal billing notification and activates a statutory Grace Period. The length of the grace period and the rules governing claim payments depend on whether your policy was purchased through the Health Insurance Marketplace with subsidies or through an individual commercial/employer plan:

1. Marketplace Plans with Advance Premium Tax Credits (APTC):
If you receive federal premium tax credits through the Health Insurance Marketplace, federal law grants a mandatory ninety (90) consecutive day grace period.
- Month 1: The plan pays all claims incurred during the first 30 days.
- Months 2 and 3: If premiums remain unpaid, your coverage is placed on 'Suspended Status.' Healthcare providers will be notified that claims are pended. The plan will hold claims without payment.
- Policy Termination: If all past-due premiums are not paid in full by the final day of the 90-day grace period, your policy is cancelled retroactively to the last day of the first month. Claims incurred during months 2 and 3 will be denied, leaving you 100% financially responsible to your doctors.

2. Commercial Individual and Employer-Sponsored Policies:
For standard commercial plans without subsidies, the contractual grace period is thirty (30) or thirty-one (31) calendar days from the billing due date.
- During this 30-day window, your coverage remains active, but overdue reminders are issued.
- If full premium payment is not received before the 30-day grace period expires, the policy is terminated effective on the original due date.

How to Restore Coverage:
To avoid policy cancellation, submit an electronic payment immediately via the Member Portal using a debit card, credit card, or ACH bank transfer. Once a policy is officially terminated for non-payment, reinstatement is rarely permitted, and you may remain uninsured until the next Open Enrollment period.
