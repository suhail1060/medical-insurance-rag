Prescription drug benefits under Apex Health Plans are managed through a comprehensive four-tier formulary designed to promote clinically effective, cost-conscious medication utilization. The formulary is reviewed quarterly by an independent Pharmacy and Therapeutics (P&T) Committee comprising licensed pharmacists and board-certified physicians.

Formulary Tier Structure:
- Tier 1 (Preferred Generic Medications): Proven generic formulations containing identical active ingredients to brand equivalents. In-network copayment: $10 per 30-day retail supply.
- Tier 2 (Preferred Brand-Name Medications): Brand-name pharmaceuticals listed on the preferred formulary guide without generic market equivalents. In-network copayment: $35 per 30-day retail supply.
- Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand-Name Medications): Higher-cost brand-name drugs with existing clinical alternatives in Tiers 1 or 2. In-network copayment: $75 per 30-day retail supply.
- Tier 4 (Specialty and Biotechnology Drugs): High-cost biologicals, oral oncology medications, and injectable proteins requiring specialized storage and clinical administration. Subject to 25% coinsurance up to a maximum cap of $250 per 30-day prescription.

Mandatory Generic Substitution: If an insured member requests a brand-name medication when an FDA-approved generic equivalent is available, the member is responsible for the standard Tier 1 copayment plus the exact difference in cost between the brand-name drug and the generic equivalent.

Maintenance Medications: Members filling maintenance medications for chronic conditions (hypertension, hyperlipidemia, asthma) may obtain a 90-day supply through the Apex Mail-Order Pharmacy program for 2.5 times the single-month copayment ($25 for Tier 1 generics; $87.50 for Tier 2 preferred brands). Specialty drugs must be dispensed through designated network specialty pharmacies and often require prior authorization and step therapy documentation.
