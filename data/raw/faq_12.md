Question: What is an Explanation of Benefits (EOB), how do I interpret its columns, and is it a bill that I have to pay?

Answer: An Explanation of Benefits (EOB) is not a medical bill. It is an accounting statement generated by Apex Health Plans that explains how a healthcare claim submitted by your doctor or facility was processed, adjudicated, and paid under your health insurance benefits.

You should review each EOB carefully to ensure accuracy before paying any bill received directly from a healthcare provider. An EOB contains several standard columns:

1. Billed Charges (Amount Billed): The gross retail dollar amount charged by the healthcare provider for the specific medical procedures rendered.
2. Allowed Amount (Plan Discount): The negotiated contractual rate agreed upon between Apex Health Plans and participating in-network providers. The difference between Billed Charges and the Allowed Amount is written off by the provider as a contractual discount and cannot be billed to you.
3. Amount Paid by Plan: The portion of the Allowed Amount that Apex Health Plans paid directly to your medical provider.
4. Total Member Responsibility: The exact dollar amount you owe directly to the healthcare provider. This figure is broken down into:
   - Copayment: Fixed fee paid for the service ($25 PCP, $50 Specialist).
   - Deductible: Amount applied toward your annual deductible requirement.
   - Coinsurance: Your percentage share (e.g., 20%) of the Allowed Amount after deductible.

5. Remark / Denial Codes: Notes explaining why a specific service was adjusted, discounted, or denied (e.g., 'Claim adjusted per contracted fee schedule').

Action Step: When you receive an invoice from your doctor's billing office, compare it directly to your EOB. Never pay a provider invoice until you confirm that the balance due matches the 'Total Member Responsibility' listed on your official Apex EOB.
