Question: How are telehealth consultations covered under my plan, what services are eligible, and what is the out-of-pocket cost?

Answer: Apex Health Plans provides comprehensive coverage for virtual outpatient consultations through synchronous real-time audio/video technology, enabling members to access medical care from home or work.

1. Designated National Telehealth Services:
Members have 24/7/365 access to board-certified physicians through our designated telehealth partner platform (Apex VirtualHealth, accessible via the mobile app or web portal).
- Covered Conditions: Non-emergency acute illnesses such as sinusitis, seasonal allergies, influenza, bronchitis, urinary tract infections, pink eye, rashes, and minor sprains.
- Prescriptions: Virtual physicians can electronically transmit non-controlled prescriptions directly to your local participating pharmacy.
- Cost-Sharing: Under the Apex PPO Gold Plan, consultations through the Apex VirtualHealth platform have a $0 copayment and do not require deductible satisfaction. Under the Apex HMO Silver Plan, visits carry a discounted $15 copayment.

2. Virtual Visits with Your Local In-Network Providers:
You may also schedule virtual visits directly with your established primary care physician, medical specialists, or licensed behavioral therapists.
- Outpatient Mental Health Therapy: Virtual psychotherapy and psychiatric medication evaluations are covered identically to in-person office visits ($25 copayment per session on PPO Gold).
- Coding and Technical Requirements: Visits must be conducted via a HIPAA-compliant interactive video platform (standard telephone calls without video do not qualify for complete virtual office visit coding).
- Cost-Sharing: Consultations with local in-network providers are subject to your standard office visit copayment ($25 PCP / $50 Specialist on PPO Gold).

Telehealth visits bypass travel and waiting room delays while accruing toward your annual out-of-pocket maximum.
