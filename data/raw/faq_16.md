Question: How does my insurance plan cover Durable Medical Equipment (DME) like wheelchairs, CPAP machines, and hospital beds?

Answer: Durable Medical Equipment (DME) is covered under Apex Health Plans when prescribed by a licensed healthcare provider for a specific, diagnosed medical condition. 

1. Definition of Covered DME:
To qualify for coverage, equipment must meet four strict criteria:
- Can withstand repeated physical use over an extended period.
- Primarily and customarily used to serve a legitimate medical purpose.
- Generally not useful to an individual in the absence of illness or injury.
- Appropriate for home use.
Covered examples include manual and motorized wheelchairs, hospital beds, oxygen concentrators, nebulizers, continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) devices, and prosthetic limbs. Non-covered comfort items include air purifiers, standard exercise equipment, and home modifications such as wheelchair ramps.

2. Coverage Rules and Cost-Sharing:
- In-Network Supplier Requirement: DME must be obtained through a contracted, in-network medical equipment supplier (such as Apria Healthcare or National Seating & Mobility). Equipment purchased from retail stores or non-contracted online vendors is not reimbursable.
- Prior Authorization: Required for any individual DME equipment purchase exceeding $750 or any equipment rental exceeding $150 per month. Your physician must submit a Certificate of Medical Necessity (CMN) documenting clinical diagnoses and mobility limitations.
- Cost-Sharing: Covered DME is subject to your annual calendar deductible, followed by a 20% coinsurance under the Apex PPO Gold Plan (or 30% coinsurance on HMO Silver).

3. Rental Versus Purchase:
Apex Health Plans determines whether high-cost equipment is rented or purchased. Typically, equipment such as CPAP machines and hospital beds are initially approved on a monthly rental basis for a trial period (e.g., 3 to 10 months) to verify compliance before transitioning to a rent-to-own purchase arrangement.
