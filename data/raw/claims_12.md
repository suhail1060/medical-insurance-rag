This policy explains the mathematical methodology and reimbursement mechanics applied to out-of-network healthcare claims under the Apex PPO Gold Plan.

Usual, Customary, and Reasonable (UCR) Benchmark:
When a member receives covered non-emergency healthcare from an out-of-network provider, reimbursement is not calculated based on the provider's retail billed charges. Instead, reimbursement is based on the Maximum Allowable Charge, determined using the Usual, Customary, and Reasonable (UCR) fee benchmark. Apex Health Plans establishes UCR rates at the 80th percentile of regional healthcare charges, as benchmarked by the independent FAIR Health healthcare database for the specific geographic zip code where services were delivered.

Calculation Methodology Example:
Consider an elective outpatient surgical procedure performed by a non-participating surgeon:
- Provider Gross Billed Charge: $4,000
- Regional UCR Maximum Allowable Charge: $2,500
- Non-Reimbursable Disallowed Excess: $1,500 ($4,000 billed minus $2,500 allowable)

Applying Plan Cost-Sharing:
Assuming the member has already met their $3,000 out-of-network annual deductible:
1. Plan Coinsurance Share: The plan pays 50% of the UCR Allowable Charge:
   $$2,500 \times 50\% = $1,250$$
   Apex Health Plans pays $1,250 directly to the member or provider.
2. Member Coinsurance Share: The member is responsible for 50% of the UCR Allowable Charge:
   $$2,500 \times 50\% = $1,250$$
3. Balance Billing Liability: Because the provider is non-participating, they can legally balance bill the member for the disallowed excess:
   $$4,000 - $2,500 = $1,500$$
4. Total Member Financial Responsibility:
   $$1,250 \text{ (coinsurance)} + $1,500 \text{ (balance billing)} = $2,750$$

Notice on Cost Accumulation:
The $1,250 coinsurance payment counts toward the member's Out-of-Pocket Maximum. However, the $1,500 balance-billed excess does not count toward any deductible or out-of-pocket maximum, illustrating the significant financial impact of choosing out-of-network care.
