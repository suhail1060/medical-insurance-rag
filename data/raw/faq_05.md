Question: What steps must I take to appeal a denied health insurance claim or an adverse pre-service authorization decision?

Answer: If Apex Health Plans denies coverage or reimbursement for a medical procedure or claim, you have the legal right under federal and state law to file an appeal. The dispute process consists of two primary stages:

Level 1: Internal Appeal:
You have one hundred eighty (180) calendar days from the date you receive the formal Explanation of Benefits (EOB) or written denial notice to file an internal appeal.
1. Review the Denial Notice: Identify the exact Claim Adjustment Reason Code (e.g., CO-50 for lack of medical necessity or CO-16 for missing clinical records).
2. Gather Clinical Evidence: Request a detailed Letter of Medical Necessity from your treating physician outlining why the requested service or treatment is clinically required. Include diagnostic test results, treatment history, and peer-reviewed medical guidelines.
3. Submit the Appeal Packet: Complete the Member Appeal Request Form (available at www.apexhealthplans.com/appeals). Submit the packet via the Member Portal or certified mail to: Apex Grievance and Appeals Committee, P.O. Box 41450, Omaha, NE 68145.

Internal Review Timelines:
- Urgent Care Appeals: Decisions are rendered within 72 hours.
- Pre-Service Appeals (services not yet rendered): Decisions are issued within 30 calendar days.
- Post-Service Claims Appeals (services already rendered): Decisions are issued within 60 calendar days.

Level 2: Independent External Review:
If Apex upholds the denial upon internal appeal and the decision involves clinical medical necessity, experimental treatment exclusions, or emergency coverage, you have four (4) months to request an Independent External Review. A third-party Independent Review Organization (IRO) will evaluate the case. The IRO's decision is final and legally binding upon the insurance plan.
