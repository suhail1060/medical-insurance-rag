Coinsurance represents the contractual percentage split of covered medical expenses between Apex Health Plans and the insured member after the applicable annual calendar deductible has been fully satisfied. Coinsurance applies primarily to major medical interventions, inpatient facility stays, outpatient surgical suites, and specialized durable equipment.

Under the Apex PPO Gold Plan, the standard in-network coinsurance ratio is 80/20. Following deductible satisfaction, the plan remits 80% of the contractual Allowed Amount directly to participating healthcare facilities and providers, and the member is billed for the remaining 20% balance. For example, if an in-network outpatient surgical procedure generates an Allowed Amount of $4,000 and the member has already met their annual deductible, the plan pays $3,200 and the member owes $800.

Under the Apex HMO Silver Plan, the cost-sharing structure is 70/30 for covered in-network hospitalizations, where the member pays 30% of the contracted rate. Under the Apex Bronze HDHP Plan, coinsurance is established at 60/40.

When an insured member elects to receive non-emergency care from an out-of-network provider under an eligible PPO contract, coinsurance is established at a 50/50 cost-split. Crucially, the out-of-network coinsurance calculation is based exclusively upon the Maximum Allowable Charge determined by regional fair-market schedules, not the provider's gross billed charges. Any remaining dollar amount billed by the out-of-network facility in excess of the Maximum Allowable Charge—termed balance billing—is the sole financial responsibility of the member and does not credit toward coinsurance limits.

All legitimate in-network coinsurance expenditures accrue toward the member's Annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum. Once this maximum cap is met, the coinsurance requirement drops to 0% for the remainder of the benefit year.
